Privacy Policy – Convey: Sweet Logistics

2026-11-17

This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the VR game Convey: Sweet Logistics (“the Game”), developed by YLVL (“the Developer”).

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Game. It does not apply to third-party platforms or services (such as Meta Quest and the Meta Horizon Store), which have their own privacy policies.

2. Data Collection

The Game does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. Headset and controller movement is used on your device while you play and is not recorded or sent anywhere. Any saved progress or settings stay on your headset and are removed when you uninstall the Game.

3. Platform Services

If you access the Game through a platform (such as Meta Quest), that platform may collect data according to its own privacy policy. The Developer does not control or access personal data collected by the platform.

4. Customer Support

If you contact the Developer (for example, by email), you may voluntarily provide information such as your email address. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties.

5. Data Security

The Developer does not store personal user data.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy: YLVL@hotmail.com
