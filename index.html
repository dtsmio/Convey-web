Convey: Sweet Logistics

A VR game for Meta Quest 3.

About

In Convey: Sweet Logistics, you are the architect of the ultimate candy supply chain. Build a complex transport system to map out the perfect route for your jellies. Enjoy a collection of cozy puzzle levels designed for a peaceful, satisfying experience.

Platform: Meta Quest 3
Developer: YLVL
